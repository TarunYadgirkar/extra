# Experiment log

Protocol: `solution/cv.py` reports two numbers per config at thresholds (0.4, 0.5, 0.6, 0.7).
- **cv**: 4-fold document-grouped CV on train real queries (train on other folds incl. CAD, score on a uniform 20k-pixel sample per held-out real query, doc-macro IoU).
- **val**: head fit on all train rows, scored on every known val pixel with the same formula as `evaluate.py`.
Select on cv first (62 docs), val second (13 docs, noisy). Default decision threshold is 0.5.
Official numbers come only from `solution/infer.py` + `evaluate.py`.

Observations
- Errors are dominated by whole look-alike negative patches being accepted (fn≈0), not boundary quality.
- Hard docs: e86e6e54f74f7d06 (sparse dashed grid on white; wide hatch variants), 796851cb2de5c8b5 (small hatched-on-gray query vs flat gray lot).
- CAD training data helps (+0.02 val on v1).
- Texture features (exp/texture): paired per-doc bootstrap on cv OOF @0.5, tx_v1f vs v3 = +0.062 [+0.036,+0.092], better on 32 docs, worse on 4 of 62. Adding x_sb to tx_v1f = -0.005 [-0.012,+0.000] (val +0.012 is noise). Blob (ink component size-class density vs query) is the strongest family; spectrum and NCC each add ~+0.01 cv on top. Extra CPU cost at inference: ~2.5s per drawing (spectra + components) + ~1.5s per query (NCC) + ~2.5s per query sampling the 4px grid.
- Residual val errors after tx: doc 796851 (queries 45/3: thin sparse hatch on gray, FPs on flat gray lot even though ncc≈0 and spectral energy ≈0 there; 28/56: FP on the same hatch rotated 90°), doc c3c44da (21/46), e86e6e (19/10). Cv worst doc cfd973b4 is symbol matching (plant circles), not texture.
- Backbone swaps are noise-level. Paired per-doc bootstrap over the 62 cv docs (exp/backbone/cv_oof.py --compare), cv@0.5 delta and 95% CI: bb_sv3-v3 +0.006 [-0.008,+0.020]; bb_sb-v3 -0.009 [-0.021,+0.001]; bb_v3s-v3 -0.005 [-0.022,+0.014]; bb_txv3-tx_v1 -0.001 [-0.014,+0.010]. Extraction per 7200x4800 drawing (MPS): DINOv2-S 0.5x+1x 6s, ViT-B 18s, DINOv3-S 6s; the extra grid pair also adds about 2s per query in features.
- Neural spatial head (exp/neural) does not beat tx_v1. On its own, the DINO-only net overfits badly: train loss falls to about 0.01, logits reach about ±15, and the output barely changes with threshold. Stacked onto tx_v1 it moves cv@0.5 by -0.013 (95% CI -0.037 to +0.006), because the GBM over-trusts the overconfident column. The best auto-context residual (nn_c3) moves cv@0.5 by +0.003 (CI -0.007 to +0.013) and val by -0.0015, which is noise. Scaling the unbounded nn_c1 correction by a factor chosen on cv (0.1 to 0.2) gives cv +0.006 to +0.009 (CI at 0.1: +0.000 to +0.012) but val -0.001 to -0.004. The dense OOF tx_v1 maps (cache/nn_gbm) reproduce bb_oof_bb_tx exactly (max abs difference 3e-8). The nn_c* cv is mildly optimistic, because each training query's map came from a fold GBM that saw the held-out fold.
- Calibration and post-processing (exp/calib). The cv column for cal_* rows scores full-image 4px probability grids of held-out-fold train real queries on all their known pixels. The grids come from four tx_v1f fold heads (cache/cal_head_f*.joblib, the same folds and params as cv.py) and are stored in cache/cal_p4. The base grids give cv@0.5 0.8908, close to the 0.8899 uniform-sample OOF, and val 0.9152, which matches the official number exactly.
- Per-query thresholds have real oracle headroom (+0.051 cv, +0.022 val), but the best threshold is bimodal: it sits near 0.05 or near 0.95 for queries whose label-free map stats look the same. A nested regressor over those stats lost 0.002 to 0.018 cv at every margin. We dropped it.
- Spatial post-processing is the win. Blur14+fill moves cv@0.5 by +0.022 [+0.011,+0.035] (better on 30 of 62 docs, worse on 5) with a positive delta in each of the four folds, and moves official val by +0.0089 [+0.0006,+0.021] (7 docs better, 0 worse). Blur alone gives +0.009 cv and fill alone gives +0.014 cv. Fill mostly recovers text and symbols inside hatched regions, because labels cover whole regions. It gives nothing on val. The hole size limit does not matter from 2.5e5 px^2 upward. Nested selection over a smoothing x fill family (median 20-60 or blur 8-20, with or without fill) gives cv +0.018 [+0.006,+0.031], so the gain survives choosing the parameters on held-out folds. A 41-way family that also includes relative min-area removal and guided filtering gives nested cv of only +0.008 [-0.009,+0.024], because min-area removal is high-variance.
- These did not help on top of blur14+fill: a global threshold of 0.4 to 0.6, hysteresis, relative min-area removal, or a guided filter with an ink-density guide (val +0.016 but cv worse than plain blur). Cross-query competition within a drawing gives a pixel to a query only when no non-alias query on the same drawing beats it. It is label-free but transductive, and it moved cv by +0.005 to +0.007 (CI touches 0) and val by +0.003. Not adopted.
- Head and training-set construction (exp/head, cache prefix hd_). The tx_v1f head sits on a plateau. Seed noise alone is about 0.003 cv (seed 1 vs seed 0: -0.003 [-0.007,+0.001]), and the deployed seed 0 is a lucky draw. Paired per-doc bootstrap vs tx_v1f at cv@0.5: doc weighting -0.004 [-0.011,+0.003], class balancing -0.002, CAD x0.5 -0.002 and x2 -0.002, all noise. Extra uniform rows hurt (-0.007 [-0.016,+0.002]). Hard-negative mining hurts in both forms: in-sample -0.018 [-0.030,-0.007], and doc-grouped inner OOF with the class prior kept -0.008 [-0.017,0.000]. The mined negatives are mostly look-alike patches that the labels treat inconsistently across documents, so upweighting them shifts the decision boundary without separating anything. Monotonic constraints cost 0.004 to 0.007. Query-constant columns carry real signal: dropping all of them costs 0.016 [-0.031,-0.005], and dropping only qw,qh,n0,n1 costs 0.006 [-0.011,-0.002].
- Doc 796851 (flat gray lot accepted for hatched-on-gray queries 3 and 45) is a tone problem, not a texture one. We set one feature at a time to the query's true-negative median and measured the drop in mean prob on the false-positive pixels. paper4_ad alone moves it by -0.62 (q3) and -0.71 (q45), followed by paper4, int4_cos and bg4_ad. The gray lot's paper level (0.827) equals the query's background exactly. In training, an exact background-tone match next to a textured query almost always means a positive, so the head ignores ncc 0 and spectral energy -19 there. No head change fixed it: abs-diff columns with monotone constraints, interaction constraints and seed bags all leave the doc at 0.53 to 0.55.
- Interaction constraints (HGB interaction_cst) give a different model rather than a better one. Alone they are +0.002 to +0.004 cv with wide CIs (about 15 docs better and 14 worse) and lose 0.003 to 0.015 on val, mostly on e86e6e and c3c44da. Averaged with the unconstrained head they add diversity: tx_v1f+icst2t is +0.0069 [+0.0014,+0.0135], and seeds5+icst2t_s3 is +0.0065 [+0.0014,+0.0122] (15 docs better, 7 worse) with row-level val 0.9163 vs 0.9147. seeds5 alone is +0.0013 [-0.0012,+0.0037] cv and +0.0037 [-0.002,+0.013] val. Each HGB member takes about 8 to 17 s to predict the 2.16M-point 4px grid of one query, so the 8-member blend adds roughly 1 to 2 minutes per query. The blend has not been tested together with the cal_pp blur+fill post-processing.

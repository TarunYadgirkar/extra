# Challenge data terms

TruTec supplies this dataset for the TruTec Hatch Matching Challenge and related non-commercial research and evaluation. You may download it, build and train solutions, evaluate them, and publish results and illustrative examples with attribution to this challenge.

These terms cover dataset assets, separately from repository code. Keep attribution when sharing examples. Do not use the drawings to identify or contact underlying projects or customers, or redistribute the dataset as a separate commercial product. No ownership of underlying third-party designs or marks is transferred. Data and annotations are supplied as-is, with incomplete supervision documented in the data card.

For other uses, contact ryan@trutec.ai. For a data concern, identify the public example ID without posting additional private information.
